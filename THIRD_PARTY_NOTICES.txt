## home_assistant_solarman

**Repository:** https://github.com/StephanJoubert/home_assistant_solarman
**Author:** Stephan Joubert and contributors
**License:** Apache License 2.0 — full text in `LICENSES/Apache-2.0.txt`

Used in:
- `lib/parser.py`, `lib/solarman_client.py` — register parsing rules and request loop (rule numbering, offset/scale, signed
  and multi-register decoding). Modified: ported to the Homey Python runtime and
  fixed two's-complement decoding of signed values (2026).
- `inverter_definitions/deye_*.json` — converted from the project's YAML
  inverter definitions; sensor names, request ranges and validation adapted for
  this app.
- `inverter_definitions/sofar_g3_hybrid.json` — based on its `sofar_g3hyd.yaml`,
  corrected and extended against SOFARSOLAR's Modbus-G3 protocol V1.42 and
  register dumps from a SOFAR ESI-12K-T1.

## ha-solarman

**Repository:** https://github.com/davidrapan/ha-solarman
**License:** MIT

Copyright © 2024 David Rapan <david@rapan.cz> and Contributors

Used in:
- `inverter_definitions/*.json` — hardware profiles (registers, scales, signs,
  lookups) were checked and updated against this project's current YAML
  profiles, the most up-to-date Solarman reference.
- `lib/fault_codes.py` — Deye "Device Alarm" / "Device Fault" bit tables,
  cross-checked against Deye's Modbus protocol V118.
- `inverter_definitions/sofar_g3_hybrid.json` — SOFAR G3 status lookup and
  register meanings, cross-checked with `sofar_g3hyd.yaml`.
